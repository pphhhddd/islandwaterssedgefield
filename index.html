The panoramic view of the lagoon straight from your private upper deck.

On the water's edge...

Experience uninterrupted lagoon views from a private nature conservation area. Set in the idyllic seaside town of Sedgefield, Island Waters offers the perfect locale for exploring the Garden Route.

The lagoon is situated directly in front of the property, with direct water access just a 50-meter stroll to the left or right. This 5-bedroom, 5-bathroom home reflects an easy-living coastal atmosphere with modern comforts.

Property Amenities: High-speed Wi-Fi (100mbps+), secure on-property parking, and backup power systems (battery for TV/Internet, load-shedding lights, and a gas hob). A baby camp-cot is available on request.
