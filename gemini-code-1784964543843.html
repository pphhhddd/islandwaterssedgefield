On the water's edge...

Set along the lagoon in the idyllic seaside town of Sedgefield, Island Waters offers the perfect locale for exploring the Garden Route, or to just relax and take in the exquisite waterfront scenery.

Within the tranquil surrounds of The Island, a nature conservation area, this 5 bedroom, 5 bathroom home in calm blues with whitewashed interiors reflects seaside style with an easy-living atmosphere.

Accommodation & Setup

The upper level consists of 3 en-suite bedrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, and a living area opening onto a lagoon-facing front deck with a dining area & braai. The lower level consists of 2 self-catering apartments.

Upper Level (Capacity: 6 adults)
  • 3 double en-suite bedrooms with lagoon and garden views
  • Fully equipped modern kitchen
  • Large open-plan living area
  • Enclosed front deck with built-in braai (BBQ)
  • North-facing back sun deck
Lower Level (Self-Catering)
  • Apt 1 (Cap: 2-3): Double bedroom, lagoon views, sleeper couch, extra single bed, kitchenette, bathroom
  • Apt 2 (Cap: 2-3): Double bedroom with lagoon views, living area, sleeper couch, bathroom

Maximum capacity of entire house: 12 people. Cable TV available. Cleaning service available on request. Strict No Smoking policy.
