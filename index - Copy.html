On the water's edge...

Set along the lagoon in the idyllic seaside town of Sedgefield, Island Waters offers the perfect locale for exploring the Garden Route, or to just relax and take in the exquisite waterfront scenery.

Within the tranquil surrounds of the The Island, a nature conservation area, this 5 bedroom, 5 bathroom home in calm blues with whitewashed interiors reflects seaside style with an easy-living atmosphere.

The upper level consists of 3 en-suite bedrooms, fully equipped kitchen, living area opening onto lagoon-facing front deck with dining area & braai. The lower level consists of 2 self-catering apartments (sleeps max 2-3 people per apartment).






Just steps away from Sedgefield's breath-taking beaches, and only 15 minutes drive to charming Knysna and Wilderness, and 30 minutes to scenic Pletternberg Bay, Island Waters is the ideal setting for your Garden Route holiday.
